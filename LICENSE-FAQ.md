# SAGE license in plain English

SAGE (Synthetic Agent Game Engine) is licensed under the **Functional Source License, Version
1.1, ALv2 Future License** (`FSL-1.1-ALv2`). The binding legal text is
[`engine/LICENSE`](engine/LICENSE). This page is a summary only. Where it and the license
differ, the license wins.

The license covers the engine only. Fablestar Expanse — its world content, lore, art, prompts
and branding — is proprietary with all rights reserved. See [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) for exactly
which paths are which.

## What you can do

- **Read, use, modify and self-host SAGE** for any purpose that isn't a *Competing Use*
  (below). That includes internal use, non-commercial education and research, and professional
  services you provide to someone who is using SAGE under the license.
- **Build and sell your own game or world on top of SAGE.**
  *Pending legal review:* this is the intent, but the license text does not name it
  explicitly. Its *Competing Use* definition includes offering something that "substitutes for
  any other product or service we offer using the Software", and Fablestar Expanse is such a
  product. Until this is confirmed, check with the licensor before shipping a commercial game.

## What you can't do

- **Use SAGE's code to build or offer a competing engine or a competing hosted service.** In
  the license's words, you may not make SAGE available to others in a commercial product or
  service that substitutes for SAGE, substitutes for another product or service we offer using
  SAGE, or offers the same or substantially similar functionality.
- **Use our trademarks**, including the SAGE and Fablestar Expanse names, beyond identifying
  where the software came from.

## When it becomes fully open source

Each released version of SAGE automatically becomes available under the **Apache License,
Version 2.0** on the **second anniversary of that version's release date**. Nobody has to renew
or re-grant anything. Release dates are recorded in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).

Fablestar Expanse content never converts; it stays proprietary.
